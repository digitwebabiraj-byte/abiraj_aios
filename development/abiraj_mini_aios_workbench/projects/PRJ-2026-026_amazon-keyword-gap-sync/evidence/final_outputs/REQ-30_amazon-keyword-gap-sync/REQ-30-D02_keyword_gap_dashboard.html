How each figure is decided. Top-Moving = more than 5 units in at least 2 of 3 months, in the requester's own “Bulbs” category · base SKU = pack size, trailing markers and account suffixes stripped, using the SKU mapping table (mapped_sku); bundles kept whole · underperforming = no sales in 6 months (counted from the product list, so listings absent from the sales report still count) or falling every month · a keyword counts as present if all its words appear anywhere in the text, any order, ignoring capitals and punctuation · top 50 terms per ASIN, terms with no purchases dropped · a pair is rejected when the two listings state a different wattage or cap fitting.
