Definitions, data sources & notes
  • Date range presets re-scope Sales, Orders, Units, Ad Spend/Sales, Conversion and New Listings to the chosen part of the month. Active Listings & Stock are always current snapshots (no per-day history). LM/LY comparison columns apply to the full month only.
  • Rows = account × marketplace. "LEDSONE UK · UK" = led_sone sold to UK buyers; its German/FR/US/IT sales are their own rows. Nothing aggregated, nothing double-counted.
  • Sales = SUM(order_total) — eBay's actual order value (product + postage actually paid), completed orders only.
  • Ad Spend / Ad Sales = eBay Promoted Listings Priority / ON_SITE campaigns only (record_subtype=ON_SITE; Standard COST_PER_SALE excluded per Thinesh). TACOS = Ad Spend ÷ total revenue · Return = total revenue ÷ Ad Spend · PPC Rank = by spend.
  • Conversion = account conversions ÷ page-views (whole-account traffic, per marketplace) · Active = distinct eBay listings on that site · New = listings created in range (ledsone listings.ebay_listings.created_at) · Sales Rank = rows ranked by revenue.
  • Stock = warehouse units for that site's SKUs — physical stock is shared across sites, so it overlaps between rows.
