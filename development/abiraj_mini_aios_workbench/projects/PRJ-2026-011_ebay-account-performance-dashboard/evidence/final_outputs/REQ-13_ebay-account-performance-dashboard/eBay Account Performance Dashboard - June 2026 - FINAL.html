Definitions, data sources & notes
  • Rows = account × marketplace. "LEDSONE UK · UK" = led_sone sold to UK buyers (£28,975.37); its German/FR/US/IT sales are their own rows. Nothing aggregated, nothing double-counted.
  • Sales = SUM(order_total) — eBay's actual order value (product + postage actually paid), completed orders only. (This corrects the earlier product+template-postage method.)
  • Ad Spend / Ad Sales = eBay Promoted Listings Priority / ON_SITE campaigns only (record_subtype=ON_SITE; Standard COST_PER_SALE excluded per Thinesh). Ad Sales is eBay-attributed at ON_SITE level (stays under real revenue at this scope) · TACOS = Ad Spend ÷ total revenue · Return = total revenue ÷ Ad Spend · PPC Rank = by spend. ACOS/ROAS on attributed sales are not shown (attribution overlaps across campaigns); TACOS is spend against real total revenue.
  • Conversion = account conversions ÷ page-views (whole-account traffic, per marketplace) · Active = distinct eBay listings on that site · New = listings created in June (ledsone listings.ebay_listings.created_at) · Sales Rank = rows ranked by revenue.
  • Stock = warehouse units for that site's SKUs — physical stock is shared across sites, so it overlaps between rows.
  • Conversion RAG threshold (green >4.5%) predates whole-account conversion (~2-3%) — recalibration pending.
